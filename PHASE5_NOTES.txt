ShopSense 2.0 — Phase 5: Smart Business Insights & Recommendations

New additions on the existing Business Insights & Recommendations page:
- Sales channel summary: orders, revenue, profit and profit margin
- Customer snapshot: unique customers, repeat customers and average order value
- Top 10 customers by revenue
- Rule-based observations based on the current filtered dataset
- CSV downloads for sales channel and customer reports

Existing pages/features from Phase 1–4 are preserved in this package.

Run from this folder:
  python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
  python -m streamlit run app.py

Important: observations are descriptive, rule-based summaries, not forecasts. Verify local behavior with your data before deployment.
